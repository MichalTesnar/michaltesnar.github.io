One thing I did too little was always talking to people. That might be a crazy statement to hear for the people that know me, cause I just talk a lot. But I think I have mastered the ability to talk without saying much, being acquainted with people I do not really know. It was point out to me by my family first, saying the blog is not much of a read if I only share the successes. Of course one cannot share everything here online, but sharing only the good things is just easier, especially if facing the public.

What I am trying to say here goes more for like sharing deeper feelings, being who you are and building deeper connections. I had very few of those outside of my home, and that is something that I am trying to change. Maybe not the easiest thing to do as an expat, but a possible thing nontheless. I am just grateful for whenever I do open myself up, make myself more vulnerable and share with other human beings. Makes just the whole experience of being here on the planet earth a lot better.
